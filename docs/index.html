Flipper

Reverse mouse scrolling on Mac, without touching your trackpad.

Flipper is a menu bar app that sets the scroll direction of a mouse and a trackpad separately. Keep natural scrolling on your MacBook trackpad and have a normal scroll wheel on your mouse, or the other way round. It's free and open source.

macOS has a single Natural scrolling setting for every device, so turning it on makes the mouse wheel scroll backwards and turning it off does the same to the trackpad. Flipper leaves that setting alone and reverses only the device you pick.

What it does

  • Reverse mouse scroll direction, trackpad scroll direction, or both.
  • Reverse vertical and horizontal scrolling, each on or off.
  • Takes effect straight away, with no restart or log out.
  • Treats a Magic Mouse like a trackpad, since it scrolls smoothly the same way.
  • Checks for updates and asks before installing anything. No analytics.

Install

Flipper needs macOS 15 Sequoia or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. It's notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other app. Download the DMG, open it and drag Flipper into Applications. It adds itself to Open at Login and asks for Accessibility, which macOS requires before an app can change scroll events.
